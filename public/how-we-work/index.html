How we work

Start with a defined business need. Agree a scope that fits the organization, available evidence and people who will carry the work forward.

01 · Understand & scope

Discuss the business trigger, systems, stakeholders and available evidence. Agree objectives, boundaries and deliverables.

02 · Assess & prioritize

Review the agreed evidence, identify gaps and dependencies, and agree practical priorities with management.

03 · Support & review

Provide the agreed outputs and support. Track actions and review progress at a cadence suited to the engagement.

A clear basis for collaboration

What you provide

A sponsor, relevant contacts, authorized evidence and time for review. Access and secure sharing are agreed before information is exchanged.

What you receive

The outputs defined in the proposal: for example a risk register, evidence index, phased roadmap or management report.

Who decides

Your executives and designated owners retain business decisions and risk acceptance. Recommendations, dependencies and unresolved questions are made explicit.

How scope changes

New systems, requirements or missing evidence may change effort and timing. Changes are discussed and agreed in writing.

Questions before getting started

How long does an engagement take?

Timing follows scope, available evidence and client review capacity. Working days and elapsed project time are agreed separately.

Does readiness support guarantee certification?

No. Certification decisions belong to an independent certification body. The engagement specifies the readiness support and evidence work included.

Can you provide hands-on implementation?

Implementation activities, access, specialist support and authorization are agreed explicitly. They are not assumed to be included in every advisory engagement.
