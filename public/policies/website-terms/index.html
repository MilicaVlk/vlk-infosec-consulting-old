Website Terms

Terms for using VLK InfoSec Consulting’s informational business-to-business website.

Last reviewed: 21 September 2026

Website operator

VLK InfoSec Consulting’s registered identity and direct contact details are provided on the Company Information page.

This website describes information security, vCISO, risk and governance advisory services. General website content is not a tailored assessment, legal opinion, certification decision or guarantee of security or compliance. Information and requirements can change; an engagement considers the organization’s actual circumstances.

Submitting an enquiry or verifying an email address does not create a consulting agreement. Scope, fees, deliverables, dependencies and responsibilities are confirmed in a separate written agreement. The website does not operate an online checkout.

You may read and link to these pages for normal business evaluation. Reuse of VLK InfoSec Consulting-authored material beyond permissions provided by law requires permission. Third-party trademarks, recommendations, standards and linked materials remain subject to their owners’ rights. References to vendors and frameworks do not imply vendor endorsement, organizational certification or accreditation.

Responsible use

Do not impersonate others, send unlawful material, introduce malware or attempt unauthorized access. Security testing requires prior written authorization. The general contact form is not an emergency incident-response channel; do not send passwords, exploit details or sensitive client evidence.

Availability and external links

Website availability is not guaranteed. External websites operate independently and their own terms apply. Any professional engagement, liability allocation and dispute arrangements are governed by the relevant agreement and applicable mandatory law. Nothing on this page excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the website changes. Updates do not amend an existing signed client agreement.
