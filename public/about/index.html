About the founder

Milica Vlk

People · Security · Progress

Milica is the founder of VLK InfoSec Consulting, an Information Security Consultant and virtual/fractional CISO. Her 17+ years span telecommunications, production systems, software development, technical leadership, project delivery and cybersecurity. Milica brings this experience to practical security priorities, governance and business decisions.

Experience behind the advisory work →

Curiosity, experience and a practical perspective

At Telekom Srbija, her work connected IP Television Services, middleware and headend platforms, followed by Radio Network Optimization and C++/MFC software development. At TeleGroup, she progressed through systems engineering, team leadership, project delivery and cybersecurity roles.

These are foundations for the advisory services now offered through VLK InfoSec Consulting; the experience described here is not presented as a record of completed vCISO or certification-readiness engagements.
