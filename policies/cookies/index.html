Cookies & Website Technologies

A source-based inventory of the current preview and the checks required before public launch.

Review draft · 12 September 2026

Prepared for this private preview. The highlighted business and processing details must be completed before these documents are used on the public business website. This draft is not an assurance of legal compliance.

What the website code currently does

A source review on 12 September 2026 found no cookie-setting code, localStorage or sessionStorage use, analytics tags, advertising pixels, third-party embeds or chatbot in the static website. Fonts and images are served as site assets. Contact fields are used in the current page to prepare an email, rather than stored by the site.

What still needs verification

This does not establish that the hosted service is cookie-free. The private preview’s login, access controls, hosting and security infrastructure can process technical data or use storage independently of the page code. A live inventory of names, providers, purposes and lifetimes has not been completed.

Future changes

Before adding optional tracking, map the actual technologies and determine applicable consent requirements. Where consent is required, keep those technologies off until a choice is made and provide an equally usable refusal and withdrawal route. A generic banner without control of the underlying scripts is not a solution.

Your browser

You can manage site storage through your browser settings. Blocking access-related storage may affect login to the private preview. No optional-cookie preference tool is currently needed by the static page code; the hosting-layer assessment remains outstanding.

This is a draft inventory, not a completed cookie audit. Finalize it against the actual public hosting, contact form and any analytics or AI features.

Related: Privacy Notice. EU ePrivacy Directive is a reference where relevant national implementing rules apply; assess the target market rather than assuming one universal cookie rule.
