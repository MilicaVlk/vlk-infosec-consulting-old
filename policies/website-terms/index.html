Website Terms

Proposed terms for using VLK’s informational B2B website.

Review draft · 12 September 2026

Prepared for this private preview. The highlighted business and processing details must be completed before these documents are used on the public business website. This draft is not an assurance of legal compliance.

Website purpose

This website introduces VLK InfoSec Consulting’s business-to-business vCISO, risk and governance advisory services. Its material is general information, not a tailored assessment of an organization’s legal obligations, security posture or eligibility for certification.

Using the content

You may read and link to these pages for normal business evaluation. Reproduction, redistribution or commercial reuse of VLK-authored materials requires permission unless allowed by law. Third-party marks, standards and linked materials remain subject to their owners’ rights. References do not imply endorsement or certification.

No engagement by browsing

Browsing the site or preparing an enquiry does not create a consulting engagement. Scope, deliverables, fees and responsibilities are confirmed in a separate written agreement. Published delivery windows are indicative and depend on scope, access and client availability.

Responsible use

Do not use the website to send unlawful material, impersonate others, distribute malware or attempt unauthorized access. The contact channel is not an emergency incident-response service. Security testing of VLK or client systems requires specific written authorization.

Accuracy and external links

Security requirements change. Check the current authoritative source and local implementing rules before relying on a regulatory summary. Linked websites operate independently. VLK does not promise uninterrupted website availability or that consulting eliminates all risk.

Contract and statutory rights

These proposed website terms do not set a blanket exclusion of liability or override mandatory rights. Liability, applicable law and dispute arrangements for professional services must be agreed in the engagement contract. This draft is designed for B2B services; a consumer offering requires a separate assessment.

Business identity and contact

Before public use, insert the full registered name, address, registration and tax details, and the required direct electronic contact. Removing clickable email links is not a substitute for applicable business-information duties.

Contact VLK · Privacy Notice

Serbian Law on Electronic Commerce, including Article 6 is relevant to the assessment of public provider information.
