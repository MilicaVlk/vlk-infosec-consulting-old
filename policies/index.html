Review draft · 12 September 2026

Prepared for this private preview. The highlighted business and processing details must be completed before these documents are used on the public business website. This draft is not an assurance of legal compliance.

These drafts are tailored to the current static website and founder-led B2B consulting model. They do not describe a chatbot, online checkout or backend lead database as already operational.

  • Privacy Notice — enquiries, rights, recipients and outstanding processing details.
  • Website Terms — informational content and business identity.
  • Terms of Engagement — proposed professional-service terms.
  • Cookies & Website Technologies — source review and hosting-layer checks.

Complete before public use

  1. Provide the registered business name, address, registration number, PIB and direct business/privacy contact.
  2. Confirm the operating start date and the entity entering client agreements.
  3. Confirm the email and hosting providers, contracts, locations and transfer safeguards.
  4. Approve retention periods, deletion procedures and access controls.
  5. Inspect cookies on the intended public host and finalize any consent controls.
  6. Review final engagement terms and update these notices before enabling new data collection.

The drafts can be reviewed now; missing facts have not been invented.
